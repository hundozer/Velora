# Legal review register

All policy pages are technical draft placeholders marked **REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW
BEFORE PRODUCTION USE**. They are not approved legal text.

Required qualified Czech/EU counsel reviews:

1. Controller identity, GDPR legal bases, Article 9 conditions, consent wording,
   rights procedures, retention and DPIA.
2. Digital Services Act role/classification, notice-and-action, statements of
   reasons, transparency reporting and internal complaint/appeal procedures.
3. **Czech AVMS / Video-Sharing Platform Classification** before significant
   adult video or livestream functionality is enabled.
4. Adult age-assurance requirements, provider selection, explicit-content
   thresholds, record minimization and safeguarding/escalation procedure.
5. Participant consent/age evidence, NCII, suspected-CSAM and law-enforcement
   escalation procedures.
6. Copyright notice, takedown, counter-notice/appeal and repeat-infringer rules.
7. Terms, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Community Guidelines, Acceptable Use,
   Content, Age, Copyright, and Moderation/Notice & Action documents.
8. Professional legal translation and review for Czech, Hungarian, Romanian,
   Slovak and German. AI-generated translations must not be used.

Future monetization has a separate legal workstream in
`FUTURE_MONETIZATION.md` and is not active.
