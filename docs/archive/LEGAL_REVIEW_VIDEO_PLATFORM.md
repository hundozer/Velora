# Video-platform legal review register

**Status: LEGAL REVIEW. This file makes no conclusion about Intimo’s classification.** Live experiences and events are technically disabled for the free MVP.

Counsel must determine whether current or planned user-uploaded video, ranking, hosting, audience reach and editorial control trigger Czech/EU audiovisual media-service, video-sharing-platform, DSA or related duties. Review must cover jurisdiction/establishment, notification or registration, protection of minors, age assurance, harmful/illegal content, reporting and redress, advertising/product placement, accessibility, transparency, regulator cooperation, recordkeeping and cross-border service.

Before enabling live/video expansion, engineering needs a signed decision record, approved risk controls, age assurance, real-time reporting and moderation, stream delay/termination, evidence preservation, uploader/participant consent, copyright tooling, transparency metrics, localized notices and incident/on-call procedures. Monetization remains a separate future gate.
