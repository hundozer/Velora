# Content policy — engineering draft

**REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW AND HUMAN TRANSLATION BEFORE PRODUCTION USE.**

Intimo is for adults aged 18+ only. Minors, suspected child sexual abuse material, exploitation or trafficking, non-consensual intimate imagery, secretly recorded intimate material, sexual violence, coercion, threats, doxxing, harassment, impersonation, scams and unlawful content are prohibited. Commercial sexual-services advertising, escort booking, incall/outcall menus and hourly service pricing are prohibited.

An uploader must explicitly confirm for each upload that every identifiable participant is an adult and consented to recording and publication. Intimo may restrict content while reviewing age or consent evidence. Consent may be withdrawn; reporting and takedown channels must remain available. Synthetic/deepfake intimate content without the depicted person’s consent is prohibited.

Visibility choices do not override the policy. Private content must never be exposed through a permanent public URL. Enforcement may include restriction, removal, warning, account limitation/suspension and preservation/escalation where legally required. Users must receive an appropriate notice and appeal opportunity unless a lawful safety or confidentiality exception applies.
