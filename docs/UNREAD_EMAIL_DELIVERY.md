## Behavior

A database INSERT trigger queues a recipient/category atomically with each new message. There is no
historical backfill. A partial unique index coalesces pending messages. The worker claims at most three
jobs with row locks and two-minute leases, then checks current unread status, both block directions,
active accounts, email preferences, confirmed Auth identity/address, and recent activity. Read/blocked/
inactive/opted-out jobs cancel; recently active members defer until ten minutes after their last activity.
Offline recipients become eligible after a two-minute settling interval. Successful sends throttle each
recipient/category for three hours; failures do not consume that allowance.

The exact provider payload is persisted before sending. Retries use the same job UUID, idempotency key
and payload, including after lease recovery or an ambiguous acknowledgement. Retry backoff is bounded,
with eight attempts and a23-hour window, shorter than Resend's24-hour idempotency retention. Jobs that
exhaust that window require operator review; restarting them blindly can duplicate mail. Provider
acceptance is recorded as SENT, not as proof of inbox delivery. Terminal payloads are cleared. Profile
erasure includes the queue, and deleting a profile cascades its rows.

Email contains no member name, message text, photo or conversation identifier. Preview links stay on
Preview. GET unsubscribe displays confirmation without changing preferences; signed POST performs an
atomic opt-out and supports List-Unsubscribe one-click clients. Account/security mail is unaffected.
