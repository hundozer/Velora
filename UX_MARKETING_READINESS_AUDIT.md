# Intimo UX and Marketing Readiness Audit

Date: 2026-08-08
Scope: preview branch only; desktop and 390 × 844 mobile checks; landing, People, and Dating public journeys.

## Outcome

The public experience is coherent, responsive, privacy-forward, and easy to understand. It is not yet ready for an unrestricted public launch because legal drafts remain visible and the live public directory has no members or dating posts. This is a conversion problem, not permission to fabricate social proof.

## What works

- The landing proposition immediately explains public browsing and privacy boundaries.
- Primary actions are clear: browse, join, or return to the dashboard.
- Mobile navigation exposes the five highest-value destinations without depending on the desktop header.
- People filters and deterministic newest-first ordering are explained in plain language.
- Dating clearly separates anonymous summaries from authenticated actions.
- Empty states are honest and actionable.

## Friction and launch risks

- An empty People directory and empty Dating page provide little reason for a first visitor to return.
- Footer links explicitly say “Draft”; this correctly prevents a false launch-ready impression but is a launch blocker.
- The value proposition is credible but needs real supply acquisition: invited pilot members, community partnerships, and transparent onboarding—not invented users or engagement.
- Six-language coverage and core authenticated journeys still require human-language and assistive-technology QA before broad marketing.

## Cost-conscious validation strategy

- Use 100 reversible, visibly fictional staging profiles to test grids, filters, pagination, and responsive density.
- Use initials/abstract placeholders; do not buy stock imagery or generate explicit content.
- Recruit a small consented pilot cohort before launch and measure onboarding completion, first profile publication, first meaningful interaction, report handling, and seven-day return.
- Do not advertise member counts, verification rates, activity, popularity, or safety outcomes without measured evidence.

## Recommendation

**CONDITIONAL GO for a closed, clearly identified staging/pilot test. NO-GO for unrestricted public marketing.** The product shell can support a pilot, but real community supply, legal approval, policy publication, and final accessibility/localization verification remain necessary.
